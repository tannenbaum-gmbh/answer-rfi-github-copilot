## Response Requirements
For each question, please provide:
1. **Direct Answer**: Clear, concise response to the question
2. **Detailed Explanation**: Additional context and technical details
3. **Citations**: Links to official documentation supporting the answer
4. **Compliance Notes**: Any relevant compliance or regulatory information
5. **Customization Guidance**: What additional information might be needed based on our specific organization

## Output Format
Format as professional business documentation suitable for:
- Formal RFI submission
- Security review processes
- Procurement evaluation
- Compliance audits

---

## Tips for Best Results
1. Copy and paste your actual RFI questions in the "Your Questions" section above
2. Include any specific context about your organization's requirements
3. Mention any compliance frameworks you need to address (GDPR, SOC 2, etc.)
4. Specify if you need responses in a particular format
5. Ask follow-up questions to drill down into specific details
